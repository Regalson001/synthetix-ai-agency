GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we build.

A few things worth knowing about AI automation and how we approach it.

What kinds of business processes can be automated?+

Common opportunities include lead capture, document processing, customer support routing, CRM updates, reporting, notifications, and repetitive data entry. The right solution depends on your tools and process requirements.

Do I need to replace my existing software?+

Usually not. Automation can often connect existing tools through APIs, webhooks, and supported integrations. We first assess what your current systems can support.

Can AI make decisions inside a workflow?+

AI can classify information, summarize content, extract fields, and recommend actions. High-impact actions should include validation, permissions, and human review where appropriate.

How do you approach data security?+

Security requirements depend on the systems and data involved. Good implementation includes least-privilege access, secure credential handling, input validation, logging policies, and appropriate provider controls.
